# GT4 Revisited 0.1.0

The first release. Gran Turismo 4 played from a disc image of your own copy, on GT3 Revisited's
engine. Nothing from the game is included. How to start: [PLAYING.md](PLAYING.md).

- The whole game from power on: the opening movie, the title, Gran Turismo mode and Arcade
- Gran Turismo mode: licenses, events, championships, Driving Missions, the dealers, used cars, tune shops, the garage and the calendar
- Arcade: Single Race, Time Trial and 2 Player Battle on a split screen
- Multi-LAN Race: up to six players on a local network, or with a session code over the internet
- Replay Theater, Photo Travel and Photo Lab
- Gran Turismo 4 Prologue's School Mode
- Driving, the computer drivers and the race rules follow the game's own, checked against races recorded on a console
- Any resolution, widescreen, high refresh rates and anti-aliasing
- Saved games are files: several games, saved by themselves, no memory card questions
- PlayStation 2 memory card images in and out, and a save editor
- Controllers and wheels with force feedback, rumble for modern controllers, DualSense triggers, remappable controls
- Mods over the disc's files, and texture packs
- Replay effects and extra views as options

Discs: Gran Turismo 4 US (SCUS-97328, v1.01) and Gran Turismo 4 Prologue (Japan, SCPS-15055).
Download: macOS on Apple Silicon.

## Known problems

- Other releases of the disc open but are not tested
- The name entry takes no typed letters: choose them with the arrows and Enter
- Photo Lab: films cannot be developed; no printer or USB memory
- Mods' cars, events and liveries are not read yet
